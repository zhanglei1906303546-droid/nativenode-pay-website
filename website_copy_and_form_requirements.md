## Part 3: Form Requirements Specification

### 3.1 Contact / Lead Capture Form (联系我们/商机采集)
**Purpose**: General inquiries and lead generation.
*   **Fields**:
    1.  **Name (姓名)**: *Required*, Text, Placeholder: "Your Full Name"
    2.  **Work Email (企业邮箱)**: *Required*, Email format, Placeholder: "name@company.com"
    3.  **Company Name (公司名称)**: *Required*, Text, Placeholder: "Your Organization"
    4.  **Industry (行业)**: *Required*, Dropdown (Options: E-commerce, Gaming, SaaS, Financial Services, Other)
    5.  **Monthly Volume (月交易规模)**: *Optional*, Dropdown (Options: <$50k, $50k-$200k, $200k-$1M, $1M+)
    6.  **Message (留言内容)**: *Optional*, Textarea, Placeholder: "Tell us about your payment needs"
*   **Success State**: "Thank you for reaching out! Our solution manager will contact you within 24 hours."

### 3.2 Demo Request Form (演示申请)
**Purpose**: Qualified leads for product walkthrough.
*   **Additional Fields**:
    1.  **Region of Interest (意向区域)**: *Required*, Multi-select (USA, Middle East, SE Asia, Europe)
    2.  **Expected Launch Date (预计上线时间)**: *Required*, Date Picker
*   **CTA Button**: "Schedule Demo" / "预约演示"
