From Sketch to Prototype: How Our In-House Design Team Fast-Tracks Your Brand

May 3, 2026 Aerlis Production Team 5 min read
Back to Insights

In the fast-paced world of fashion, the time between a "brilliant idea" and a "physical product" is your most valuable asset. Many bag OEM factories require you to provide complete technical packs before they even start. At Aerlis Leather, we take a different approach. Our in-house design and sampling team acts as an extension of your brand, turning simple sketches or mood boards into retail-ready prototypes in record time.

1. The Conceptualization Phase: Beyond the Sketch

Every great bag starts with a vision. Whether you have a professional CAD drawing or a hand-drawn sketch on a napkin, our design team begins with a Technical Feasibility Review.

We don't just copy your drawing; we optimize it for manufacturing. We look at:

  • Structural Integrity: Will the bag hold its shape with the chosen leather?
  • Ergonomics: Are the strap placements comfortable for the end-user?
  • Proportional Balance: Does the hardware size complement the overall dimensions?

Design Insight: Small adjustments in corner radius or seam construction during the sketch phase can often reduce material waste by 15%, lowering your unit cost without changing the aesthetic.

2. Speed-to-Market: The 7-Day Prototype

While many factories take 3–4 weeks for a sample, Aerlis Leather has optimized the handbag prototype workflow to just 7 working days. This efficiency comes from three core pillars:

  • Dedicated Sample Workshop: Our sampling room is separate from the main production line, meaning your development never gets "stuck" behind a 10,000-unit bulk order.
  • In-House Master Pattern Makers: Our senior pattern makers have 20+ years of experience. They can translate a 2D design into a 3D structure with pinpoint accuracy on the first try.
  • On-Site Material Library: We maintain thousands of leather swatches and hardware samples in-house, eliminating days of waiting for supplier deliveries.

3. The Importance of "Master Patterns" in OEM Production

The prototype isn't just a one-off sample; it's the Master Pattern for your entire future production. If the pattern is flawed, every bag in your 1,000-unit order will be flawed.

During our bag development process, we create digital patterns that ensure:

  • Consistency: Every unit in the bulk order will be identical to the approved sample.
  • Material Efficiency: Precision cutting patterns reduce leather waste, directly lowering your manufacturing cost.
  • Scalability: Once a master pattern is locked, we can scale production from 100 to 10,000 units without losing detail.

4. Why an In-House Design Team is Your Competitive Edge

Partnering with a bag OEM factory that has an in-house design team offers major advantages:

  • Reduced Communication Gaps: Designers and makers sit in the same room, solving technical problems in minutes rather than days of back-and-forth emails.
  • Cost Optimization: We can suggest alternative construction methods during the design phase that save you money without compromising the look.
  • Confidentiality: Your original designs stay within our walls, protected by our strict Intellectual Property (IP) protocols.
