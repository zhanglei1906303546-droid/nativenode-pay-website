Part 1: The 2026 Shipping Landscape

The global shipping market has entered a new phase of "Digital Reliability." Buyers now expect real-time visibility and multiple routing options based on urgency and budget.

1. Air Freight: The Speed King

For high-value leather goods or time-sensitive seasonal launches, Air Freight remains the gold standard. In 2026, we've seen a stabilization of rates, though premium "Express" services (3-5 days) still command a higher price. It is the ideal choice for **Sample Customization** and initial "test" batches.

2. Sea Freight: The Matson & ZIM Advantage

For bulk production, Sea Freight is indispensable. Strategic use of "Fast Sea" services like Matson from China to the US West Coast can reduce transit times to just 12-15 days—nearly rivaling traditional air cargo at a fraction of the cost.

Part 2: Navigating HTS Codes & Tariffs

The **Handbag Import Duties** you pay are determined by your Harmonized Tariff Schedule (HTS) code. A single digit difference can result in a 10% swing in cost.

1. Premium Leather Goods (HTS 4202.21)

Genuine leather handbags typically fall under 4202.21. While subject to base duties, they often avoid some of the higher "synthetic material" penalties. However, US buyers must still account for Section 301 "China Tariffs" unless an exclusion is active.

2. Synthetic & Textile Bags (HTS 4202.22)

Bags made of plastic sheeting or textile materials have different duty structures. In 2026, many "Vegan Leather" materials (like Cactus or Apple leather) require careful classification to ensure they are not unfairly penalized under outdated plastic categories.

Part 3: The DDP Revolution for Mid-Size Brands

One of the biggest shifts in 2026 is the rise of **DDP (Delivered Duty Paid)** manufacturing. In this model, Aerlis Leather handles everything: manufacturing, export, ocean/air freight, customs clearance, and final delivery to your warehouse.

For brands looking to eliminate "Tariff Shock," DDP provides a fixed, predictable price. It removes the need for you to find a separate customs broker and ensures that all **US Import Duties** or **EU VAT** are handled correctly at the point of origin.
