About the Company

A Trusted Partner for
Premium Leather Goods

Founded in 2010, Guangzhou Airlisi Leather Goods Co., Ltd. (广州市艾尔丽思皮具有限公司) is a leading OEM/ODM leather goods manufacturer based in Guangzhou, China — the heart of China's leather manufacturing industry.

Over 15 years, we have built a reputation for exceptional craftsmanship, rigorous quality control, and on-time delivery. Our state-of-the-art factory spans 8,000 m² and employs 200+ skilled artisans and engineers dedicated to turning your designs into world-class products.

  • Full OEM & ODM capability — from design sketch to finished product
  • Strict 3-stage quality inspection process
  • Minimum order quantities as low as 100 pcs per style
  • Sample customization before bulk order confirmation
  • Dedicated account manager for every client
