In the fast-evolving fashion industry, finding a Leather Bag Manufacturer is easy, but finding a strategic partner is a challenge. 1. R&D Speed and Sample Customization

In 2026, the market waits for no one. A top-tier OEM Bag Factory must bridge the gap between a design sketch and a physical product within days. At Aerlis, our R&D center specializes in Sample Customization. We don't just follow blueprints; we optimize them for manufacturing. 2. Mastering Sustainable and Genuine Materials

Material compliance is no longer optional. A professional China Leather Goods Supplier must offer a diverse portfolio:

  • Genuine Leather: Sourced from top-tier tanneries with full traceability.
  • Sustainable Alternatives: We are leading the way in Vegan Leather Manufacturing, using recycled PU and plant-based fibers to meet the growing demand for eco-conscious luxury.
  • Hardware Durability: Using salt-spray tested, anti-oxidation hardware that ensures your bags last for years, not months.

3. Rigorous Quality Control (The AQL 2.5 Standard)

Quality is a promise, but QC is a process. For any Private Label Bag Manufacturer, the standard should be non-negotiable. Our factory implements a 3-stage inspection:

  1. Incoming Material Inspection: Every hide and zipper is tested before cutting.
  2. In-line QC: Monitoring stitching precision and reinforcement at stress points.
  3. Final Pre-shipment Inspection: Using the AQL 2.5 standard to guarantee excellence for every bulk order.
